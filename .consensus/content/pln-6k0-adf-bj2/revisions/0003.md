# Plans and Subplans

Plans often grow in complexity and have the potential to break out into Subplans to address a specific feature.

A Subplan appears as a child to another plan. This relationship is organiational only and each Subplan operates as a full Plan in its own right, including having additional Subplans under it.

When a prompt context is developed for a Subplan, it will include context for its parent's Plans.
