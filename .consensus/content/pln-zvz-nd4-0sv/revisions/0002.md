# Step 1: Scaffold and Threads

The first step of plan UI Components: the component scaffold and the first extraction, Item Thread Filters and Item Threads (items 6 and 7 of the parent). The app behaves as before, except where a decision below says otherwise.

## The scaffold

A component is an AngularJS .component(), one folder per component under public/components/<name>/, holding <name>.js (the registration and its controller), <name>.html (the template, loaded by templateUrl, no build step) and <name>.css (its styles, one link tag each; consensus.css keeps the page, the palette and the shared classes). index.html becomes a skeleton of component tags. A component takes what it shows through bindings or the State service, reports through events, and its header comment names both: that is its interface. Names follow the CLAUDE.md exception: Angular's camelCase at registration, kebab-case tags, one-word bindings where possible, nothing else camelCase.

## Item Thread Filters

The "Waiting on ..." and "Highlight ..." dropdowns above the thread list. Changes are broadcast to the other components (the Html and CodeMirror views, the thread list) so they update their thread displays. Filters are global and apply to all items, not just the selected one.

Decided: the Waiting-on filter becomes global like Highlight, remembered in the browser, and the per-tab Filter goes (the guide's Tabs section changes with it). A change is a State event (highlight-changed, and a new filter-changed) that the html-view, the CodeMirror editor and the thread list listen to. The dropdowns are a thread-filters component and stay above the thread list.

## Item Threads

The list of thread cards according to the Thread Filters. Each card has components within it:

1. Thread Header: thread name, state, waiting on, anchor text, and delete.
2. Thread Transcript: the transcript of thread messages with owners and timestamps, rendered markdown as an Html view.
3. Thread Actions: a text input box and buttons for Replying, Resolving, etc.

Decided: the reply box and the compose box are an editor of the kind the Editor setting names, Monaco or CodeMirror, in a plain mode: markdown, word wrap, no line numbers, no anchors, no preview, no save. Five lines high to start, with a drag handle on the bottom edge; the dragged height is remembered in the browser, one height for both boxes. One instance per place, mounted when its box appears: one for the reply form (shown on the selected thread only) and one for the compose card. The reply draft stays kept per thread (State.Drafts). The compose card is a thread-compose component of its own, beside thread-card with its thread-header, thread-transcript and thread-actions; the inline delete confirmation stays in the header, the collapse controls in the transcript.

Until Step 3 adds CodeMirror and the Editor setting, the box is Monaco.

## Decided before the build

1. Loading. Each component registers on the Consensus module with .component( name, { templateUrl: '/components/<name>/<name>.html', controller, bindings } ). index.html has one link tag per component stylesheet in its head and one script tag per component after the services (after app.js). Express serves public as it is; Angular fetches each template once and caches it. No manifest, no template cache priming.

2. The components of this step and their bindings:
   - thread-filters: no bindings. The two dropdowns and the count; reads State.Filter and State.Highlight and sets them through State.SetFilter and State.SetHighlight. The per-participant filters come from State.Participants.
   - thread-list: no bindings. The compose card and the thread cards in the pane's order (detached first, then by position, whole-document last), the "no threads" line, and the selection moving on after your own reply or resolve.
   - thread-card: thread. The card's classes (state, selected, detached, mine), the click that selects, and inside it thread-header, thread-transcript and, when selected, thread-actions.
   - thread-header: thread. The state badge, the detached badge, waiting on, the anchor words (a click scrolls to the passage), and delete with its inline confirmation.
   - thread-transcript: thread. The replies with their owners and times, the collapse controls, and the resolved and applied records. Collapsed replies stay in State.Collapsed.
   - thread-actions: thread. The comment-editor over State.Drafts[ thread.Id ] and the buttons: Reply or Reply and Contest, Reply and resolve, Resolve, Re-anchor, Start a new Subplan.
   - thread-compose: no bindings. State.Compose: the anchor words or "the whole document", the comment-editor over State.Compose.Text, and Comment, Comment and resolve, Start a new Subplan, Cancel.
   - comment-editor: text (two-way, =), placeholder (@), autofocus (@). The plain-mode editor box used by thread-actions and thread-compose; it edits the draft in place, as the textarea does now.

3. The requests. Post, Reply, ReplyAndResolve, Resolve, Delete and Reanchor move out of the controller into a Threads service (public/js/threads.js becomes it), with Busy and the reload after each. The components call it; nothing else holds thread requests. move_on, the selection passing to the next thread, stays with thread-list, which knows the order.

4. The global filter. State gains Filter remembered under its own key (default Waiting on me) and SetFilter, which remembers it and broadcasts filter-changed. tabs.js drops the per-tab Filter: the restore when a tab is shown and the watch that writes it back; a Filter left in a remembered tab is ignored. The read view listens to filter-changed as it listens to highlight-changed, a listener for the html-view and the CodeMirror editor of the later steps.

5. The comment-editor over Monaco. The Editor factory gains Plain( Element, Text, OnChange ), creating an independent Monaco instance (the Edit view's instance stays as it is): language markdown, word wrap on, no line numbers, no glyph margin, no folding, no minimap, no current-line highlight, no scroll past the end, the page's theme, following consensus-theme as the big editor does. The component creates its instance in $onInit and disposes it in $onDestroy; the reply form and the compose card are ng-if, so there is one instance per place. Ctrl+Enter in the box presses the card's primary button (Reply, or Comment).

6. The height. Five lines to start: five times the editor's line height plus its padding. A six-pixel drag bar under the box; dragging sets the height (the editor lays out as it moves, a minimum of two lines) and remembers it in the browser under one shared key, so both boxes open at the last dragged height. The remembered height is of the look, not of a server.

7. Styles. The threads blocks of consensus.css (.threads-top, .thread and its badges, .thread-anchor, .reply*, .record, .compose) move to the stylesheets of the components that use them: thread-filters.css, thread-card.css, thread-header.css, thread-transcript.css, thread-actions.css, thread-compose.css, comment-editor.css. The threads aside itself and its width stay in consensus.css with the layout.

8. index.html. The threads aside becomes <thread-filters> and <thread-list>; the ThreadsController and its markup go. Nothing else in the page changes in this step.

9. The smoke, at the end: the filter changes the list and survives a switch to another tab and a reload; Highlight still changes the read view; compose, Comment, Reply, Reply and resolve, Resolve, Re-anchor and Delete work as before; the reply draft survives selecting another thread and back; the drag bar resizes and the height comes back after a reload; the theme switch recolours the boxes; Ctrl+Enter posts. The 180 node tests run untouched.
