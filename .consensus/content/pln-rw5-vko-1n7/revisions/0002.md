# Review 2026-10-29

In the beginning there was Desktop and then there was Server. Confusion occured and then Desktop was split into Sidecar and Client. There are now 4 different editions of Consensus, each with different roles and expectations.

While I do wish to maintain a clear separation between the editions, instructiions were given to abstract out similar sections and componentize them for reuse between the editions. Sidecar and Client are more recent creations and have established the pattern of reusability. Desktop and Server have not been updated and may contain outdated or contrary code.
